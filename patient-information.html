Your first visit, made easier.

A little preparation can help your appointment run smoothly. Here are a few useful things to know before visiting the practice.

01 / GET STARTED

Register your details

Contact reception to ask about registering as a new patient and whether any forms should be completed before your appointment.

02 / BRING ALONG

What to bring (if applicable)

  • Medicare card or DVA Veteran Card
  • Current medication list
  • Relevant test results or medical records
  • Concession or Healthcare Card.
03 / BE PREPARED

Before you arrive

Please arrive with enough time to check in. Let reception know when booking if you need an interpreter, accessibility assistance or additional time for your appointment.

Helpful tip: If you have several concerns to discuss, mention this when booking so reception can help arrange a suitable appointment.

Fees & Medicare

Consultation costs depend on the practice’s billing policy and the service provided. Please ask reception about any fee before your appointment. Everbright Medical’s specific fees and bulk-billing eligibility are not confirmed in this page. Please contact the practice for its current billing policy.

Billing typeWhat it means
Bulk billing The provider accepts the applicable Medicare benefit as full payment for the service. Ask whether your appointment and circumstances are eligible.
Other services Some procedures, forms, reports or certificates may have separate charges and may not attract a Medicare rebate. Confirm the cost beforehand.

What about Medicare rebates?

A Medicare rebate is the benefit paid towards an eligible medical service. For privately billed appointments, the rebate may cover only part of the fee, leaving an out-of-pocket gap.

  • Ask about the consultation fee before the appointment.
  • Check whether the service is eligible for a Medicare rebate.
  • Confirm payment methods and when payment is due with reception.

Appointment Cancellation Policy

Need to cancel or reschedule?

If you can no longer attend, please contact the practice as early as possible. Giving notice helps the team manage appointments and may allow another patient to be seen.

  • Contact the practice as soon as you know you cannot attend.
  • If you are running late, phone the practice for advice.
  • If you need another appointment, ask reception to help arrange a suitable time.

Late cancellations & missed appointments

Appointment times are reserved for patients. Late cancellation or failure to attend without notice may incur a fee. Please contact us to check whether a fee may apply in your circumstances.

Plans changed? Let the team know as soon as you can. No specific cancellation fee or notice period is stated here because the practice’s exact policy has not been confirmed.

Get to know MyMedicare

MyMedicare is a government program. Benefits depend on eligibility and program rules. Please refer to the official Australian Government information for current details.MyMedicare is a voluntary Australian Government patient registration model that helps formalise your relationship with your regular general practice and GP.

A more connected approach to primary care

Registration is free for eligible patients. It lets your chosen practice know that you have selected them as your regular care team. Benefits include greater continuity of care and access to longer Medicare-funded telehealth consultations with your GP where the relevant program requirements are met.

    Who Can Register?

  • You must have a valid Medicare card or DVA Veteran Card.
  • You must meet the current MyMedicare eligibility requirements.
  • Your chosen general practice must be registered with MyMedicare.
