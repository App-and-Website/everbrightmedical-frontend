Our Introduction

About Everbright Medical

At Everbright Medical, we believe quality healthcare starts with understanding people, listening to their concerns and treating every individual with care and respect. Based in Kurri Kurri, NSW, we are committed to supporting the health and wellbeing of individuals and families through accessible, patient-focused healthcare.

From routine health assessments and preventive screening to ongoing support for chronic conditions, we aim to help our patients make informed decisions about their health. We understand that every person has different needs, which is why we value clear communication, a welcoming environment and care that considers each individual's circumstances. Whether you are attending a general health assessment, seeking support for your family or managing an ongoing health concern, Everbright Medical is here to help you take positive steps towards your wellbeing.

Commitment to Care

Your Needs, Our Priority

Putting Patients First Through Personalised Care

We recognise that good healthcare is not simply about treating health concerns; it is about making sure every patient receives the right attention, clear guidance and appropriate care. We place importance on meaningful consultations, taking the time to understand individual circumstances and considering each patient's needs when discussing healthcare options. By encouraging open conversations and informed choices, we aim to make every visit a positive and reassuring experience.

Our focus extends beyond individual appointments to the wider picture of health and wellbeing. We value preventive healthcare, regular health monitoring and continuity of care as important parts of supporting better health over time. Whether addressing a specific concern or helping patients stay on top of their general health, we strive to make quality healthcare people we serve.

Dr Sharon Liu

General Practitioner, MBBS, FRACGP

Dr Sharon Liu is a General Practitioner with a clinical background spanning both general practice and emergency medicine. She has several years of experience in general practice, following three years of working in emergency medicine across multiple hospitals within the Hunter New England region. Her diverse clinical experience has given her the opportunity to manage a broad range of health concerns, from acute presentations to ongoing medical conditions and preventive healthcare.

Dr Sharon values the continuity of care that general practice offers and enjoys building lasting relationships with her patients. She believes in taking the time to listen, understand individual health concerns and provide thoughtful, patient-centred care. Having lived in Australia for over ten years, she is proud to call Newcastle home and enjoys being part of the local community.

Outside of medicine, Dr Sharon is an enthusiastic reader and avid book collector, often finishing an entire book in a single day. She looks forward to welcoming patients to Everbright Medical & Dental and supporting their health and wellbeing through every stage of life.
