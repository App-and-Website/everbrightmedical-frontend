Frequently Asked Questions

What healthcare services do you provide?

We offer a range of healthcare services, including antenatal care, baby checks, men's health, cancer screening, children's health, chronic disease care, diabetes management and general health assessments.

Do I need an appointment before visiting?

We recommend booking an appointment before visiting to help us plan your consultation and reduce waiting times. Please contact our clinic for assistance.

Do you provide healthcare for children and babies?

Yes, we provide baby checks and children's healthcare services to support their growth, development and overall wellbeing.

Can I discuss ongoing health concerns with a GP?

Yes, you can arrange a consultation to discuss ongoing health concerns, review your symptoms and explore appropriate healthcare options.
